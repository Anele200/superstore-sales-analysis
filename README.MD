 Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

  Project Overview

This project analyses Superstore transactional sales data from 2015–2018 using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights by analysing sales trends, regional performance and product category performance.



  Business Problem

Superstore has a large volume of transactional sales data, but raw data does not immediately show which regions, categories and periods are contributing most to overall sales.

This project aimed to answer key business questions such as:

- How do sales change over time?
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which region generates the lowest sales?
- Which product category generates the highest sales?
- How many transactions were recorded across each region?



  Tools & Skills

- Data Cleaning

- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Data Visualisation
- Business Analysis
- Insight Generation



  Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw dataset was prepared in Microsoft Excel before analysis.

Key preparation steps included:

- Cleaning sales values
- Creating a `Sales_Clean` field
- Creating a `Date_Clean` field
- Converting dates into a usable Excel date format
- Validating sales calculations
- Preparing the dataset for PivotTable analysis
- Grouping dates by year and month for trend analysis



  Analysis Performed

 1. Monthly Sales Trends

Monthly sales were analysed across the 2015–2018 period to identify changes and fluctuations in sales performance over time.

The analysis showed considerable month-to-month variation, with stronger sales levels appearing in the later years.



 2. Sales by Region

Regional sales and transaction volumes were analysed to compare performance across the four regions.

| Region | Sales | Transactions |
|---|---:|---:|
| West | $710,219.68 | 3,140 |
| East | $669,518.73 | 2,785 |
| Central | $492,646.91 | 2,277 |
| South | $389,151.46 | 1,598 |
| Total | $2,261,536.78 | 9,800 |



 3. Sales by Category

Sales performance was also analysed across the three main product categories.

| Category | Sales | Share of Total |
|---|---:|---:|
| Technology | $827,455.87 | 36.6% |
| Furniture | $728,658.58 | 32.2% |
| Office Supplies | $705,422.33 | 31.2% |
| Total | $2,261,536.78 | 100%|



  Key Insights

- Total sales were approximately $2.26 million across 9,800 transactions.
- The West region generated the highest sales at approximately $710K.
- The South region generated the lowest sales at approximately $389K.
- Technology was the highest-performing category, generating approximately $827K.
- Sales fluctuated considerably from month to month between 2015 and 2018.
- Sales were relatively well distributed across the three product categories.



  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several areas could be investigated further:

1. Investigate the factors contributing to the lower sales and transaction volume in the South region.
2. Analyse Technology sub-categories and individual products to understand what drives its sales performance.
3. Investigate factors behind monthly sales fluctuations, including seasonality, promotions and product performance.
4. Compare sales with profit and profit margins in a future analysis to understand whether high sales translate into strong profitability.
